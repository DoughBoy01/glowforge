# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users

Men 35+ tracking skin performance over time. They arrive on the home screen in
short, repeat visits — most often on a phone, often at night — to answer two
questions: *is this working?* and *what do I do today?* They are not shopping
for a beauty routine; they are checking a scoreboard. Many are gym-literate and
read this the way they read a lifting log or a game stat screen.

### Brand Personality

**Tactical, competitive, nocturnal.** The product is a training log, not a
beauty routine (see the tone note in `src/lib/products.ts`). Copy is blunt and
imperative. The interface should feel like a HUD you check mid-mission — a
readout with stakes — not a wellness dashboard.

Emotional goal: *momentum*. The user should feel they are running a mission with
a number worth defending, not filling in a self-care checklist.

### Aesthetic Direction

Named direction: **"GlowForge Tactical"** (`src/app/globals.css`). Dark-first —
near-black violet base (`#07060D`), neon magenta primary (`#FF2E88`), cyan
accent (`#00E5FF`) reserved for data/trust, static CRT scanline wash over the
whole app.

Reference: **Grand Theft Auto** — specifically its Vice City / GTA VI neon
register and its HUD. What that means concretely here:

- Big italic uppercase display type for the thing that matters (mission-title
  treatment), not for chrome.
- HUD readouts: segmented meters, mono tabular numbers, tight tracking-wide
  caps labels.
- Skewed/angular blocks and corner-notched panels instead of uniform rounded
  rectangles.
- Neon sign script (Yellowtail) as a rare signature flourish.
- Motion that reads as a system booting up: staggered reveals, count-ups,
  sweeps.

Anti-references: wellness/spa softness, pastel gradients, rounded friendly
SaaS dashboards, anything that looks like a habit-tracker template.

### Type System

Four families, each with one job (`src/app/layout.tsx`):

| Family | Token | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Anton (italic caps) | `font-heading` | Display. The mission number and section titles. |
| Inter | `font-sans` | Body copy only. |
| Roboto Mono | `font-mono` | Readouts: labels, stats, tabular numbers. |
| Yellowtail | `font-script` | Neon-sign flourish. Rare — logo and signature moments only. |

### Design Principles

1. **Spend the identity where it counts.** Anton and Yellowtail exist; a screen
   that never uses them is off-brand by omission. The single most important
   number on a screen gets the display face, not the body or mono face.
2. **Hierarchy through contrast, not containers.** Not every block needs a card.
   Vary panel treatment — full-bleed, notched, bare — so the page has rhythm.
3. **Every readout is a HUD element.** Numbers are mono and tabular; their
   labels are caps with wide tracking. Meters are segmented, never smooth
   progress bars.
4. **Neon is punctuation.** Magenta means "you're winning / act now". Cyan means
   data and trust. Both lose all meaning if everything glows.
5. **Motion on arrival, not on idle.** One orchestrated staggered entrance beats
   scattered perpetual animation. Everything respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Hard Constraints

These come from `CLAUDE.md` and outrank aesthetic preference:

- Face age is measured against the user's **own baseline**, never their real
  age, and **only a scan moves it**.
- Face age has no absolute "good" value — never attach a `ScoreLevelBadge` to
  it. Direction is carried by narrative copy and `DeltaBadge lowerIsBetter`.
- Never use `primary` for the "on track" score tier — this palette's magenta is
  too close to `destructive`. `ScoreLevelBadge` uses `accent`.
- Check both light and dark. Dark is primary, but light must not break.
